Martinelli’s 90+6’ Stunner: Brazil Overcomes Japan in Dramatic Round of 32 Thriller

Sports Desk | GenZ Frontier | June 30, 2026

HOUSTON, TX — Brazil once again proved why they remain the pulse of world football, snatching a heart-stopping 2–1 victory over Japan in the dying seconds of their Round of 32 clash at Houston’s NRG Stadium.

In a match that pushed the five-time world champions to their absolute breaking point, it was Gabriel Martinelli’s 90+6th-minute finish that finally broke Japanese hearts and sent the Seleção soaring into the Round of 16. Brazil once again proved why they remain one of football’s greatest powers, snatching a dramatic 2-1 victory over Japan thanks to Gabriel Martinelli’s stoppage-time winner in the FIFA World Cup Round of 32.

Japan shocked the Seleção by taking the lead in the first half through Kaishu Sano, leaving the five-time world champions chasing the game. Brazil struggled to break down a disciplined Japanese defense before veteran midfielder Casemiro restored parity early in the second half with a towering header.

With extra time looming, Brazil continued to pile on the pressure. Their persistence finally paid off in the 90+5th minute, when Martinelli calmly finished from close range after a perfectly timed assist from Bruno Guimarães, sparking jubilant celebrations among Brazilian players and supporters.

The dramatic victory keeps Brazil’s dream of lifting a record sixth FIFA World Cup alive and secures their place in the Round of 16. While Japan exits the tournament with heads held high after an outstanding performance, Brazil once again demonstrated the resilience and winning mentality that have defined their footballing history.

For Brazil, it was more than just a victory—it was another reminder that the Seleção never stop believing until the final whistle.

Final Score: Brazil 2–1 Japan Goals: Casemiro (56’), Martinelli (90+5’) — Kaishu Sano (29’)

A Tactical War of Attrition

Japan arrived in Houston with a game plan defined by discipline and tactical courage. They shocked the NRG Stadium crowd in the 29th minute when Kaishū Sano capitalized on a defensive lapse to slot home a clinical finish, forcing Brazil to fight from behind for the majority of the match.

Brazil struggled to unlock the Samurai Blue’s low block throughout the first half, facing a defensive structure that seemed impenetrable. However, Carlo Ancelotti’s half-time adjustments signaled a shift in tempo. The equalizer arrived in the 56th minute when veteran anchor Casemiro rose highest to power home a towering header, reigniting Brazil’s momentum.

The Late-Stage Magic

As the match edged toward the prospect of extra time, Brazil’s persistence remained unwavering. With Japan visibly fatiguing under a relentless second-half onslaught, the opening finally arrived deep into stoppage time.

Following a perfectly weighted assist from Bruno Guimarães, Gabriel Martinelli found space at the back post, calmly slotting the ball home to trigger delirium among the Brazilian supporters. The goal was the ultimate demonstration of the Seleção’s "never-say-die" attitude.

What’s Next for the Seleção?

The victory serves as a massive psychological boost for Brazil, who have now overcome the historical "comeback curse" that has plagued their tournament runs since 2014. By fighting back from a goal down to win, Ancelotti’s side has proven they possess the mental fortitude required to survive the knockout stages.

Brazil now advances to the Round of 16, where they await the winner of the high-stakes clash between Norway and the Ivory Coast. While the journey to the Hexa remains long and fraught with challenges, tonight’s performance serves as a stern warning to the rest of the world: Brazil is far from finished.
